Right-now feed
Stay informed with live, continuously updated readings of your car’s internal temperature. The feed refreshes in real time, instantly alerting you when heat levels begin to rise so you can act before it becomes unsafe.
Temperature monitoring
Accurately track and visualize your car’s internal temperature in Fahrenheit. The system monitors fluctuations, highlights danger zones, and provides clear insights to help you maintain a safe environment inside the vehicle.
Instant communication
When critical heat levels are detected, automatic alerts are sent through phone calls or messages to your selected contacts. This rapid response ensures immediate awareness and quick action to prevent heat-related risks.
